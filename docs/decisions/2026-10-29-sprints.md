# Sprints

A turn delivers one slice end to end and, measured over a six-turn build of a
public social network, costs what one engineer-week costs in a pre-AI team:
one screen or one relation with its oracle, tests, four reviews and a live
walk. That is the unit an LLM and a founder both size the same way, so the
plan is written in turns and converts to engineer-weeks one to one.

Nothing above the turn existed. The journal's "periodic brief refresh" named a
cadence without a trigger; the acceptance ledger was walked one turn's lines at
a time and the earlier ones trusted; the mechanisms a turn learned stayed in
its dispositions; and nothing read across turns for the seams between slices.

## The decision

A sprint is four feature turns and a wrap, run as `/pronto:sprint`
(`commands/sprint.md`). The wrap is half a turn and owns seven things: the
brief refresh with the next four turns made concrete, journal collection, one
walk of the whole ledger with two sessions where a line needs two people, a
snapshot release and tag, a cross-turn pass by the product and architect
seats, the harvest of mechanisms into `llms.txt` and seams into `PENDING.md`,
and one cost line. The sprint keeps no file: its opening line is the brief
entry and its closing line is the harvest.

## Why four

Ten turns between wrap-ups is a quarter of engineer-weeks; the plan is stale
and the journal unreadable long before. One turn is a turn's own Rung 4 done
twice. Four is a month, which is where a refresh, a collection and a re-plan
cost less than the drift they remove. A four-person team's sprint lands three
to five engineer-weeks of shipped feature, so four turns also matches the
sprint a reader already pictures.

## Why the harvest is per sprint

A reference edited after every turn tilts toward whatever broke last. Four
turns of dispositions, read together, separate a mechanism from an incident:
the filtered delete that resolves against the public table, the server region
that re-reads only after this client's own write, the embed read whose rows
never enter the collection. Each recurred or generalized across turns before
it earned a sentence in the reference.

## Rejected

- **A sprint file.** A second source of truth for where the plan is, which is
  the thing the turn refuses for the same reason.
- **A velocity number.** One turn is one engineer-week by definition; the cost
  line records what varied, which is the only number the next plan needs.
- **Wrapping every turn.** Rung 4 already walks the turn's lines and writes
  its entry; the wrap's value is in what spans turns.
