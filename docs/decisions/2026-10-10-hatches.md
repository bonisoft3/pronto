# Hatches

**Status: proposed.** The grammar changes below are not built. The external-effects
record ([2026-10-03](2026-10-03-external-effects.md)) built the one hatch that carries a
record; this decides what it shares with the others and what the compiler owes each.

## The question

The platform already asks one question everywhere, under different names: a retried
form, a CDC redelivery, a second clock, a crashed worker's re-entry, a time-travel replay
are all *a second delivery*. `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, `Idempotency-Key`,
`uuid5(schedule, instant)`, `resolution=ignore-duplicates`, the `refused` arrow, the
re-entry ladder are all answers to it. This record names the question — **what happens
if it happens twice** — and derives from it the three views an app's impurity has:
where the row is, where the code sits, and what delivery it can claim.

## As of now

What the schema holds, in the order one thinks about it ([schema.cue](../../schema.cue),
[machine.cue](../../../omnishell/machine.cue), [objects.ts](../../objects.ts)).

**Entities.** `#Entity` is five things that matter — `durability`, `fields`, `access`
(server rows only), `validations`, `seed` — plus `sync` and `writers`, and a long tail of
schema evolution (`id`, `ordinal`, `retired`, `generated`, `indexes`, `uniques`,
`invariant`, `checks`, `enums`, `bounds`). Everything that decides a row's tier, roles,
sync and level follows from `durability`:

| durability | the row lives | `server` | `access` | level (`#DurabilityEffectLevel`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tab` | this tab | no | — | 1 ephemeral |
| `device` | this device | no | — | 1 ephemeral |
| `offline` | the server, a copy cached | yes | required | 2 compensable |
| `live` | the server, read live | yes | required | 2 compensable |
| `server` | the server only | yes | required | 3 replicated |

**ir.html.** Eleven kinds (`objects.ts` `KINDS`): `entity`, `pipeline`, `screen`, `state`,
`flow`, `test`, `decision`, `handler`, `validation`, `hatch`, `unit`. A mecha machine and an
external effect — the most consequential declarations a program makes — have no kind to
land on; `state` is a screen's lifecycle wireframe, not a chart's state.

**Machines.** One chart shape, two interpreters. The terminal's
([machines.md](../../../omnishell/docs/machines.md)): a transition is
`{guard?, target?, assign?, effect?: {level?, op, entity, …}, raise?}`. The cluster's
(`#MechaMachine`): `{on?, after?, invoke?, entry?, exit?}` with actions
`{assign?, effect?, raise?}`, run inside the write by a `BEFORE` trigger. The terminal's
`effect.level?` is declared and read by nothing; the cluster's effects carry no level at
all. Beside the relational ops the cluster admits three more things under the word
`effect`: `call` (a plpgsql function), `notify` (`pg_notify`), `stream` (a signal to a
pipeline).

## The axis: the level is the durability

The effect levels of [REFERENCE.md](../../../omnishell/REFERENCE.md#effects) are not five
kinds of write. They are durability, extended by one rung at each end — *no row* below,
*a row nobody here holds* above — and each rung is one answer to the question:

| level | the row written lives | if it happens twice | formal model | the obligation, and who discharges it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 projection | nowhere | nothing: it is a function | a function | no ambient authority — `jessie.ts`, the compartment |
| 1 ephemeral | `tab`, `device` | the same register, one writer | a Mealy machine over an event log the owner holds | the log replays — the battery, the Postman tour |
| 2 compensable | `offline`, `live` | the server may refuse; the speculative row is withdrawn, never undone by arithmetic | optimistic concurrency with abort-only compensation (Kung & Robinson 1981); a one-step saga | the chart draws `refused` — `machineLint` |
| 3 replicated | `server` | a join: `x ⊔ x = x`; out of order, it commutes | a join-semilattice (CRDT, CALM); inside the trigger, a serializable transaction | a lattice op on the feed; `UNIQUE` in the transaction |
| 4 external | outside the cluster | the peer answers: by key, by query, by a human, or by accepting the duplicate | durable execution: a deterministic function replayed over a history of recorded outcomes; the saga's pivot | the ledger claims before the call; the re-entry is declared |

Two algebras, then, and an axis of ownership. Level 0 is functions. Levels 1–4 are all
join-semilattices, differing in whose lattice and where it lives: a register you alone
write; the server's lattice with your speculative rows as an overlay (`refused` is the
overlay being dropped, which is why compensation never subtracts); the server's lattice
itself, where `ensure` and `upsert` are joins and `accumulate` is one only when keyed by
the event; and the ledger, a grow-only set of attempts (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` is
union), which is what turns an act into a level-3 row while the peer stays opaque.
"Twice" is one law, idempotence of join; "out of order" is its sibling, commutativity;
replay by recompute is CALM: a monotone program converges from any delivery order.

**Below the hatch the platform is monotone.** The hatch is where monotonicity stops — a
sent mail cannot be un-joined — and the ledger is how the *attempt* is pushed back into
the monotone world even when the peer is not. Composition is lattice product: a machine
over several entities lives in the product of their lattices, held where the farthest one
is held, so a chart's level is the `max` over the durabilities its arrows write and a
screen's or an app's is the same fold. **Nothing above an entity declares a level.** The
only places an author states a crossing are `invoke` (level 4, with its re-entry) and a
hatch (with its why).

Level 3 is one level, not two. Inside the trigger's transaction atomicity absorbs the
duplicate, so `insert` strict is admitted; on the change feed delivery is at-least-once
and unordered, so only joins are. The platform already enforces half of this unnamed —
crud adds `Prefer: resolution=ignore-duplicates` to every pipeline write
([proxy](../../../../libraries/mecha/docs/proxy.md)) — and the other half is one lint: a
pipeline admits `ensure`, `upsert` and keyed `accumulate`; strict `insert` and unkeyed
`update` are the trigger's. Helland's ACID 2.0 is the reference for why; it is not a
level.

**Sources are the inbound face of the same axis.** A question may be asked twice; an act
may not be performed twice ([rows, time and effects](../../../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-rows-time-and-effects-design.md#questions-and-acts)).
A question is keyed by content and its recording — the `Move` row, the oracle's capture —
makes the second ask free; an act is keyed by identity and is level 4. The clock, a click,
a schedule's cron and an engine's answer all land as rows of an append-only set.

## Code: seats, ports, adapters

Code has no level. The level belongs to the row a write lands on, and app code never
writes: it describes, and the platform performs — the terminal's interpreter at 1–2,
mecha's interpreter (the `BEFORE` trigger; "guard" is XState's word for a condition and
is retired for the trigger) at 3, the airlock at 4. A unit of code is therefore specified
by two things, and its level is read off the second.

**Seat** — how far it can reach *without* the platform. The ladder is the web platform's
own: SES **compartment** (a sub-realm, no ambient authority), **iframe** (a new realm:
its own DOM, same-site, no storage, no network beyond the app), **worker** (a new agent:
a thread, `fetch`, IndexedDB), **container** (a new process). Each rung adds exactly one
authority. Every Jessie role sits in a compartment implicitly; a vendored compartment is
the same seat with audited non-Jessie code; the airlock is the platform's own container,
whose ports are the ledger and the declared binding
([the seats](../../../omnishell/docs/terminal.md#the-seats)).

**Ports** — everything it touches, as entities and events: `reads` (rows handed in),
`raises` (events to charts, which then write at the entity's level), and for a container
`writes` (the role it connects as and the tables its grants reach; mecha's interpreter
still runs the chart on them, since the trigger is `SECURITY DEFINER`). The platform's
side of a port is an **adapter** — the `postMessage` bridge, the Dapr binding, the crud
role — which is Cockburn's ports and adapters, Erlang's ports to code outside the VM, and
the component model's world of imports and exports, and is why the Jessie `adapters`
role has the name it has.

A seat sets a ceiling: a compartment can only be level 0; an iframe presents and raises;
a worker may *ask* — it is a question, and must name the entity its recording lands in;
a worker that acts on a third party is refused, since an act from N tabs is N acts and
acts are server-only structurally. A container may act, and is a hatch.

## Delivery, derived

The question's answer has a standard name, and it is derived, never declared:

| `delivery` | at level | derived from |
|---|---|---|
| `exactly-once` | 0–3; 4 with `idempotent` or `reconcile` | a function; one writer; a join; the key or the query |
| `at-most-once` | 2; 4 with `unknown` | the `refused` arrow; a human closes it |
| `at-least-once` | 4 with `{repeat: why}` | the duplicate is accepted, for the stated reason |
| `unspecified` | a hatch | nobody answers; the author signs a `why` |

The level-4 re-entry ladder is delivery spelled out, and the level names are the saga's:
`compensable` is the compensatable step, `replicated` the retriable one (a join may be
re-run freely), `external` the pivot — the point of no return, which is why it alone
needs the ledger. `ephemeral` is the local step every saga paper leaves implicit.

## Hatches

**A hatch is a performer the platform did not build**: an app-authored artifact that
reaches past level 0 without the platform performing the crossing, so its delivery is the
author's to answer or nobody's. The platform owes it nothing on replay — it runs as
declared — and that is what distinguishes it from an effect, which carries a record.
Two kinds, and the rows that are not hatches but are often mistaken for them:

| | reaches | delivery | record | replay | obligation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sql`, `proto` | level 3, *inspection withheld* (squawk, buf cannot read it) | unspecified | none | re-runs it | `files`, `why`; refused if it loads an extension that does I/O — that is level 4 wearing 3's clothes |
| `container` | level 4 in the cluster | unspecified | none | runs as declared | `ports.writes` (role, tables), `egress`, `why` |
| an external effect | level 4 | derived from its re-entry | the ledger | answers from the ledger | not a hatch: the platform performs it |
| `{repeat: why}` | level 4, at-least-once | declared | the ledger and the why | answers from the ledger | the one effect carrying prose, as a hatch does |
| a vendored `worker` | the browser's network | `exactly-once` as a question | an entity | answers from it | `record`, `ports`; not a hatch while it only asks |
| a vendored `iframe`, `compartment` | present and raise | derived from the chart handling its events | none | re-runs | `ports`; never a hatch |
| `call` (plpgsql) | whatever the function does | unspecified | none | runs as declared | **a `sql` hatch**, not an effect |
| `notify`, `stream` | a port | — | — | — | **raises**, not effects |
| inline `<script>`, `on*=`, `javascript:`, a static with code | unbounded, undeclared | none | none | — | **refused**: the markup rule and the door's `Content-Security-Policy` with `require-trusted-types-for 'script'` are the fence that makes "only four seats exist" true at runtime |

Tests are outside the axis: a test is not a delivery and never happens twice in
production. Platform code is the performer, not a hatch; its correctness is the
platform's own suite.

## What a program declares, and what is derived

Nothing in the entity grammar changes. The authored additions are the ports on a
vendored unit, `record` on a worker, and `why` on a hatch; everything else is a view.

```cue
// declared today, unchanged
entities: Tick: durability: "tab"
handlers: metronome: {of: "board", src: "shell/handlers/bump.js", note: "the beat after this one"}

// declared: a question in a worker (today `capabilities: []`)
vendored: stockfish: {isolation: "worker", src: "shell/units/stockfish/unit.js", files: [...]
	ports: {reads: ["Game"], raises: ["engine_move"]}, record: "Move", note: "asked for a preference, never a possibility"}

// declared: the two hatch kinds
hatches: "odds-history": {kind: "sql", files: ["migrations/013_odds_history.sql"], why: "a window query squawk misreads; it loads no extension"}
hatches: "odds-rust":    {seat: "container", ports: {writes: ["TeamRating"]}, egress: ["api.football-data.org"], why: "a Rust simulation pronto does not look inside"}

// derived, one shape every check reads
#Reach: {level: 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4, seat?: string, ports: {reads: [...], raises: [...], writes: [...]},
         delivery: "exactly-once" | "at-least-once" | "at-most-once" | "unspecified",
         record: string, replay: "recompute" | "answer from record" | "run as declared"}
// "machine/beat":    {level: 1, delivery: "exactly-once", replay: "recompute"}            // max over its arrows
// "source/stockfish": {level: 1, seat: "worker", delivery: "exactly-once", record: "Move", replay: "answer from record"}
// "hatch/odds-rust": {level: 4, seat: "container", delivery: "unspecified", replay: "run as declared"}
```

The XState DSL is constrained, never extended: `effect.level?` leaves the chart grammar
because the level is the entity's, and no new key enters a transition.

## What ir.html shows

Two kinds join the eleven: `machine` and `effect`. An entity renders as a Mermaid
`erDiagram` and a machine as a `stateDiagram-v2`, both derived from `program.cue` by the
compiler — Mermaid is already pinned in every ir.html head and `realworld` already draws
`flowchart`s by hand; the derivation makes the picture the bijection checker's, not the
author's. The app's summary is three columns derived by the fold — level, weakest
delivery, hatches — so an app's whole impurity is reviewable on one line, each row
traceable to a declaration someone signed.

## The cleanup this needs first

1. Derive `level` from the entity in both interpreters; drop `#Effect.level?`; the
   `refused` rule becomes total. One durability table, not two.
2. Give `machine` and `effect` an ir kind and a renderer.
3. Reclassify `call` as a `sql` hatch and `notify`/`stream` as raises on a port.
4. Move `#Entity`'s evolution fields under one key so the entity reads as its five
   lines; fold `writers` and `sync` into the ports vocabulary.
5. Rename the trigger from "guard" to mecha's interpreter in docs and `emit.cue`.
6. The fence: `check markup` refuses scripts and inline handlers; the door emits CSP
   with Trusted Types; `vendored.capabilities` becomes `ports`.

## Rejected

- **A flat `hatches.kind` enum** (`sql | proto | container`): it names three things with
  nothing in common but the word; the level and the record are what a reviewer needs.
- **A `twice` field of the author's**: the answer is derivable everywhere but the hatch,
  and a declared answer that disagrees with the derived one is a lie the compiler cannot
  catch. `delivery` is derived; only the hatch writes `why`.
- **ACID 2.0 as a second level 3**: the distinction is real and is one lint about place,
  not a rung on the axis.
- **Five algebras**: 1 and 2 are protocols (a single-writer register, OCC with abort);
  pretending otherwise would be false precision. Two algebras and an axis of ownership
  is both true and enough.
- **A fifth effect "with no guarantees"**: an effect without a ledger is what `container`
  already is; giving it the effect vocabulary would lose the one property that makes an
  effect replayable.
- **Code with a level of its own**: code describes; a level on code would let a handler
  claim what only the row it writes can decide.

## Prior art

Sagas (Garcia-Molina & Salem 1987; the compensatable / pivot / retriable classification);
ACID 2.0 (Helland & Campbell, *Building on Quicksand*, 2009; Helland, *Idempotence Is Not
a Medical Condition*, 2012); CRDTs (Shapiro et al. 2011), CALM (Hellerstein & Alvaro
2020), LVars; optimistic concurrency (Kung & Robinson 1981); durable execution and the
recorded side effect (Temporal); exactly-once sinks in stream processing (Flink,
Kafka); algebraic effects and handlers (Plotkin & Pretnar) for "describe, then a handler
performs", and effect theories for the laws; ports and adapters (Cockburn 2005), Erlang
ports, the WebAssembly component model's worlds; object capabilities (Miller, *Robust
Composition*, 2006) and SES for the seats; command–query separation (Meyer) for
questions and acts; ECMAScript realms and agents for the isolation ladder.

## Open

- **A question's recording across tiers.** A worker's record is an entity; a cluster-side
  question (an LLM, an FX rate) has no declared home yet — the airlock's ledger is for
  acts, and a question keyed by content wants a different table.
- **Tier-aware hatches.** A container's `egress` is a compose network today; at k8s or
  Cloud Run it is a policy the deployment writes, and the record should say which.
- **The fence at the door.** CSP with Trusted Types breaks any library that builds HTML
  from strings; the terminal already forbids them, but a vendored iframe's content may
  not, and the header applies per origin.
