# Ops

Product: High information density, keyboard-first speed, and unambiguous refusal and validation copy. Optimize for the daily operator scanning hundreds of rows, not a marketing landing page.

Architect: Strict multi-tenant RLS isolation, audit-trail durability, and idempotent CDC rollups that survive WAL replay without drift. Every statutory rule belongs in the schema or lattice, not UI conventions.

Engineer: Scannable tables, forms, and charts with zero silent truncation, strict focus order and ARIA semantics, and deterministic locale/number formatting across appearances.

QA: Exercise boundary and malformed inputs, permission-denial paths, multi-user tenant isolation, and rollup reconciliation under repeated events.
