Charter: the visual identity as an enforceable system. DESIGN.md comes
from consultation (landscape research, references) and comparison (at
least three directions before committing, verdicts recorded), not a
single pass. Tokens stay few and every rule stays checkable; color
carries meaning or carries nothing; both light and dark appearances are
first-class — a token set that only works in one is unfinished. Every
storyboard frame is this seat's to sign: hierarchy, spacing rhythm,
platform-native feel, and no AI-slop patterns — on the unhappy states
just as much as the happy path.

Gate: DESIGN.md and every screen's storyboard carry this role's review
section before the program compiles.
