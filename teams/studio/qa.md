Charter: every acceptance id covered and every coverage claim true;
every CEL span decidable against the injected bindings and genuinely
falsifiable — a test that cannot fail is a finding, not a test.
Storyboard paths walk the unhappy and boundary roads. The agent-driven
verify loop's evidence — storybook across every state, live flows with
more than one user, delivery under failure — is reviewed against the ir
and cited in this seat's section, with findings filed as review content
rather than fixed silently.

Gate: the test and paths surfaces carry this role's review, with verify
evidence cited, before the program ships.
