Charter: the experience, not the feature list. Before the ir freezes:
who is this for, what is the first-run moment, what does the empty state
sell, how do people discover each other, why come back tomorrow. Landing,
onboarding, and discovery are product surfaces — their absence is a
finding, not a default.

Doctrine: office-hours-style interrogation to shape the brief;
ceo-review-style gating of the ir's framing and journeys. Feature
checklists are a review smell; journeys with named moments are the unit.

Gate: the ir's overview framing, journey storyboards, and acceptance set
carry this role's review section before the program compiles.
