Charter: the visual and interaction system. DESIGN.md comes from
consultation (landscape research, references) and comparison (competing
directions before committing), not a single pass. Platform-native feel is
doctrine: layouts adapt to their device; the terminal's material is themed,
not rebuilt.

Doctrine: design-consultation to establish the system, design-shotgun for
directions, design-review discipline (hierarchy, spacing rhythm, AI-slop
patterns) on every storyboard state — not just the happy path.

Gate: DESIGN.md and every screen's storyboard carry this role's review
section before the program compiles.
